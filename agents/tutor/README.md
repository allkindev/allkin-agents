# Tutor

A patient private teacher, for you or your children: a language, accounting, statistics,
programming, a chapter of history, the tool you must use at work, the exam in June. Tutor does not
hand you a page to read: it finds out where you stand, explains at your level, makes you practise,
checks that you really understood, and brings back what you learnt before you forget it.

## What it does

- **A first interview and a diagnosis**: your goal, your time, then a few real questions to find
  your starting point.
- **A plan** from where you are to your goal, in steps that are things you will be able to do.
- **Sessions that make you work**: one new thing at a time, an example before the rule, exercises
  just above your level, questions you can only answer if you understood.
- **Corrections that teach**: it points to where it goes wrong and lets you find it — hints
  first, the answer last — and looks for the wrong idea behind a mistake.
- **Spaced review**: each session starts with what is due; what you recall is pushed further
  away, what you miss comes back.
- **Sheets to keep**: one page per notion — the idea, an example, the traps, three questions.
- **A record** per learner and subject: the plan, the progress, what to review and when.

It helps you do your homework; it does not do it for you.

## What it needs

An imported agent arrives with no right beyond the defaults. On its Agent page:

| Setting | Why |
|---|---|
| **Memory** (on by default) | To remember who learns what, and what works with each learner. |
| **Can search and read pages on the web** (optional) | To verify what it teaches and find the official syllabus of an exam. |
| **Can schedule actions** + competence **Manage actions** (optional) | To propose a review session at regular times. You approve each action. |

It needs no system access and no service.

## How to use it

1. Say what you want to learn and by when. It asks a few questions (one form), then tests where
   you stand.
2. Read the plan and adjust it.
3. Come back for sessions of twenty to forty minutes: it knows where you stopped. Say "let's
   review" for a session of recall alone.
4. Give it your course or a past exam paper as an attachment: it works from them.

For a child, sit with them the first time: tell Tutor their first name and school year.
