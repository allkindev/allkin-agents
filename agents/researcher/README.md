# Researcher

Finds out and keeps watch. Ask it a question and you get a dated brief whose every claim has its
source. Give it a subject to follow — competitors, prices, a technology, a regulation — and it
comes back to it week after week, telling you only what is new.

## What it does

- **Research**: opens the pages instead of summarising search results, goes back to the primary
  source, cross-checks what matters, and writes the answer first — with its confidence, what is
  contested and what it did not find.
- **Watch**: keeps, for each subject, the sources to return to and what it already reported. Each
  run compares and reports the new only; "nothing new" is one line.
- Works on its own through Allkin's Actions: a weekly watch, a price checked every morning.
- Never states what it did not read: no invented figure, address or quotation.
- Separates fact, claim and opinion, and dates everything.

## What it needs

An imported agent arrives with no right beyond the defaults. On its Agent page:

| Setting | Why |
|---|---|
| **Can search and read pages on the web** | Indispensable: without it, it cannot research. |
| **Has a browser of its own** | For pages a simple fetch cannot read: prices loaded by script. It does not need “Can act on the pages”. |
| **Can run sub-tasks** | To run several lines of research side by side. |
| **Can schedule actions** + competence **Manage actions** | For a regular watch. You approve each action it creates. |
| **Competences: Research brief, Topic watch** | Its two methods; downloaded when you give them. |
| **Memory** (on by default) | To remember what it watches and the sources you trust. |

## How to use it

- **A question**: "Compare the three main invoicing tools for a five-person company in France:
  price, e-invoicing compliance, exports." You get the brief, with sources.
- **A watch**: "Follow what our three competitors publish and change in their prices; tell me every
  Monday." It sets up the watch with you, proposes the action, then reports each week.

## Good to know

Some sites refuse automated visits. Researcher does not try to get round them: it tells you and
uses another source.
