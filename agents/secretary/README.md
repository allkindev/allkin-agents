# Secretary

Your administrative assistant. It writes what must be written, fills in what must be filled in,
remembers what was promised and to whom, and reminds you before it is late.

## What it does

- **Letters and e-mails** ready to send, in the right register and the conventions of the
  recipient's country, on your template when you have one.
- **Minutes** of a meeting from a transcript or rough notes: decisions, actions, owners, dates.
- **Word and Excel documents**: fills a form or a template without touching its layout, builds a
  table, checks the totals. The documents are changed in place with Allkin's Office tools.
- **A follow-up list**: what waits on you, what others owe you. It adds to it whenever you mention
  a commitment, tells you what is due when a conversation opens, and writes the chasing message.
- **Reminders and a weekly review** through Allkin's Actions.
- **An address book** of the people you write to: their name as they spell it, how to address
  them, their language.

It never invents a name, an amount or a date: what is missing is asked for, or left clearly marked.

## What it needs

An imported agent arrives with no right beyond the defaults. On its Agent page:

| Setting | Why |
|---|---|
| **Competences: E-mail drafting, Meeting notes, Typography** | Its methods; downloaded when you give them. |
| **Can schedule actions** + competence **Manage actions** | For reminders and the weekly review. You approve each action it creates. |
| **Memory** (on by default) | To remember how you sign, your details, your habits. |

It needs no system access and no web. It sends nothing itself: it hands you the text.

## How to use it

1. Set **the work folder** of the conversation (the folder pill of the send bar) to the folder of
   your papers if you want it to read and fill your own files.
2. Ask as you would ask an assistant: "Answer this letter from the insurer and refuse the
   increase", "Make the minutes from these notes", "Fill the quote template for Dupont, 12 hours at
   85 €".
3. Mention what is pending as it comes; ask "what is waiting?" whenever you want.
4. Give it your letterhead and your standard letters once: it keeps them as templates.
