# Community Manager

Runs your social networks like a professional — X, LinkedIn, Instagram, Facebook, Threads, YouTube,
TikTok, Mastodon, Bluesky, Pinterest. It keeps an editorial line and a calendar, writes each post
once and adapts it to every network, triages comments and drafts the answers in your voice, and
measures what worked. Nothing is published that you did not approve.

## What it does

- **The editorial line**: one interview at the start — who you speak to, about what, in which
  tone, what you never say — written down and checked against every post.
- **The calendar**: the weeks planned, the holes and the collisions shown, nothing published
  twice.
- **Posts**: a master text, then a version per network — length, hook, hashtags, link, picture
  format — checked by scripts against each network's limits. You see each one as it will appear.
- **The inbox**: comments and mentions sorted by urgency, answers drafted; complaints moved to
  private, trolls left alone.
- **Reports**: figures computed from the data, per period, with what to keep and what to stop.
- **A watch** on what is said about you and your field.

## What it needs

An imported agent arrives with no right beyond the defaults. On its Agent page:

| Setting | Why |
|---|---|
| **Competence: Community management** | Its manual and its scripts (lengths, threads, calendar, triage, statistics). The scripts run in a closed frame: they read and write its own folder, nothing else. |
| **Competence: Typography** | For clean text on every network. |
| **Services: your networks** | To read and publish there. Every publication shows you a card. Without a network's service, it hands you the text and you publish. |
| **Can schedule actions** + competence **Manage actions** | To schedule posts and the weekly planning. You approve each action. |
| **Can search and read pages on the web** | For the watch. |
| **Memory** (on by default) | For your tone and your habits. |

## How to use it

1. Open a conversation: it starts with the editorial interview (one form) and writes your line.
2. Ask "plan next week": it proposes subjects, you keep some, it writes and adapts the posts.
3. Approve each post; it publishes through the network's service, or you copy the text.
4. Ask "what is waiting in the comments?" and "the report of the month" when you want.

## Good to know

It never invents a figure or a testimony, never buys or fakes engagement, never uses a picture
without the right to, and never answers on a sensitive subject without you.
